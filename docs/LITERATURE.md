# Literature Notes

- H. Henzinger and R. Milner, *On Relating Bisimulation and Simulation*. Foundational paper connecting game-based reasoning with process equivalence.
- L. Aceto et al., *Modal Logic and Bisimulation Games*. Provides a gentle introduction to spoiler/duplicator dynamics.
- J. van Benthem, *Logic in Games*. Explores logical perspectives on adversarial graph processes.
- A. Dawar, *Pebble Games and Finite Model Theory*. Relates pebble games to bisimulation and logical indistinguishability.

These resources offer accessible background without requiring deep familiarity with model theory proofs.
