# Literature Overview

## Bisimulation and Modal Logic
- Milner, R. *Communication and Concurrency*. Introduces bisimulation in process calculi.
- Hennessy, M. & Milner, R. (1985). *Algebraic laws for nondeterminism and concurrency*.
- Stirling, C. (1995). *Modal and Temporal Properties of Processes*.

## Game-Theoretic Verification
- Stirling, C. & Walker, D. (1995). *Local Model Checking in the Modal Mu-Calculus*.
- Jancar, P. (1995). *Decidability of bisimulation equivalence for pushdown processes*.
- Seidl, H. (1996). *Fast and simple computation of bisimulation equivalence*.

## Algorithmic Enhancements
- Plaisted, D. (1983). *A recursively defined ordering for proofs with operators* (transposition tables in search).
- Kocsis, L. & Szepesvári, C. (2006). *Bandit based Monte-Carlo Planning* (MCTS/UCT).
- Silver, D. et al. (2016). *Mastering the game of Go with deep neural networks and tree search*.

## Symmetry and Automorphisms
- McKay, B. D. & Piperno, A. (2014). *Practical graph isomorphism, II*.
- Babai, L. (2016). *Graph isomorphism in quasipolynomial time*.

## Learning Heuristics in Games
- Tesauro, G. (1995). *Temporal Difference Learning and TD-Gammon*.
- Sutton, R. & Barto, A. (2018). *Reinforcement Learning: An Introduction*.

These resources provide context for the techniques implemented in this project.
